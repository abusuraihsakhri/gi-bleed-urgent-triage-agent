CLINICAL REFERENCE · OFFLINE CALCULATOR

Upper GI bleeding risk scores

Calculate Glasgow–Blatchford (GBS) and optional AIMS65 from explicitly assessed findings. Calculations run in your browser; no data are sent to a server.

Clinical decision support only. Not a diagnostic device or an automated triage, treatment, or discharge decision. Suspected active bleeding or hemodynamic instability requires immediate clinical assessment regardless of score.

Results

Enter assessed observations to calculate a score. No simulated data or automatic clinical actions are used.

Interpretation safeguards

GBS 0–1 identifies patients who may be eligible for outpatient follow-up after clinician assessment. It does not establish stability or justify discharge by itself.

AIMS65 is a prognostic score; this application does not assign unsupported absolute mortality probabilities.
